# Artwork provenance

The bats, pumpkins, ghost, spiderwebs, and blood drips are original SVG paths and CSS shapes authored directly for this Halloween mockup/theme. They were not downloaded from an icon library, stock-art service, or a public-domain collection. `artwork.svg` contains the editable symbol definitions; each display HTML embeds a copy for reliable local rendering and animation.

The theme source and SVG artwork are supplied under the same license choice as CRG ScoreBoard: **Apache-2.0 OR GPL-3.0-or-later**. They are not being represented as public-domain assets. There is no third-party Halloween-art attribution requirement. System fonts are used, with no new bundled font files.

Excluded from the installable package:

- The prototype's YouTube video `CqAdIQfeeTE` and thumbnail from `Q8n08y98iK0` were preview references supplied by the user. They are third-party media and are not claimed to be public domain or licensed by this theme.
- Any AI-generated concept image from the design discussion is not used in the shipped theme.
- Example sponsor banners and example team names are not shipped. Real sponsors are loaded from the scoreboard's configured media; their rights remain with their owners.

The artwork contains no coffins or religious symbols.
